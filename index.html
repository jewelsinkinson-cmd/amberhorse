Training Offered

Find the right starting point

Every horse and partnership is different — these are the areas we work through together, from ground work to daily care.

Ground Skills + Liberty Work

Build rapport and communication from the ground up — gait maintenance, softness, transitions, directional changes, and ground tying without constant correction.

Riding with Connection

Refine your seat, hands, legs, and energy for soft communication under saddle — turns, side-passing, backing, and maintaining gait on a loose rein.

Problem Solving

Short-term, targeted lessons for specific behavioral or training issues, starting with a full evaluation of your horse and goals.

Trail and Travel Preparedness

Build a confident trail partnership — obstacle training, emergency stops, confidence-building, resolving barn-sour behavior, trailer loading, and hauling.

Handling & Management

Practical, day-to-day care that works — herd dynamics, feeding aggression, and building consistency and structure at home. Reach out with your specific needs for a customized plan.

More about Connected Horsemanship

Amber's love for horses was immediate and indulged by her grandparents at a young age.

She spent her early years as a DIY horse owner & dedicated apprentice to a dirt-road breeding operation, riding and helping care for half-feral horses that Horse & Rider Magazine couldn't have prepared her for.

She continued with these barely handled horses throughout her adolescence, learning "old school" methods and significantly through trial & error.

As a young adult, she was eventually introduced to Natural Horsemanship & began studying & training with a Master Trainer in a NH program.

This approach proved to fill many holes in the previous learning she had been a student of for so many years. Many lightbulbs went on & Amber started to share her experiences.

She began helping rehab OTTBs for rehoming through a non-profit. This community presence led to forming & facilitating a large trail riding & event group as a Trail Coach.

Soon she was onto becoming a Certified Riding Instructor with CHA, giving one-on-one arena & trail lessons, trail rides, facilitating Youth Horse Camps & much more.

Her training methods have evolved over the years, into less-invasive techniques that approach desired change, considering the whole horse.

Although teaching & sharing the life-changing work of natural, Connected Horsemanship is her passion, she's spent significant time pursuing variety in other areas as well; patterned speed events, competitive cow work & a little jumping as the highlights. She is committed to continuing her education through many avenues, studying the principles of other kind-minded trainers, LIMA, Attunement, as well as, biomechanics, cow work training, liberty work clinics, obstacle course challenges, ranch work, & Stock Horse and Ranch Riding shows.

No matter the discipline or sport, there's always room for Connected Horsemanship!
