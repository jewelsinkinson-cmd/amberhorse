Training offered

Wherever you are, we'll meet you there

Whether it's riding lessons, ground work, liberty, or a specific problem to solve, you'll walk away with techniques to train your horse yourself. You'll see the benefit of your new skills from the very first session.

Handling and management

For an everyday routine with your horses that runs smoother and feels more rewarding. If herd dynamics, feeding aggression, inconsistency or lack of structure are getting in the way, reach out so we can put a plan together.

Ground skills and liberty work

Build real rapport and two way communication.

  • Maintain gait and direction without micromanaging
  • Softness in the face and body
  • Drive and draw, on line and at liberty
  • Smooth upward and downward transitions
  • A whoa that actually yields results
  • Ground tie for grooming, saddling and mounting

Riding with connection

Put ground skills into practice. Rely on energy and aids to communicate under saddle, respectfully.

  • Check in before you mount
  • Riding the whole horse, not just their face
  • Refined seat, hands, legs and energy
  • Turns on the front end and hindquarters, side passing, straight backing

Problem solving

Need short term lessons for a specific issue? Reach out for an evaluation and we'll build a plan around it.

Trail and travel preparedness

Build a confident trail partner.

  • Keeping your horse focused on you
  • Obstacles and emergency stops
  • Building confidence and calming a nervous horse
  • Barn or buddy sour behavior
  • Trailer loading and safety
  • Hauling, including hooking up, driving and backing
Connection is key.

Amber's love for horses was immediate and indulged by her grandparents at a young age. She spent her early years as a DIY horse owner and dedicated apprentice to a breeding operation down a dirt road, riding and helping care for half feral horses that no magazine could have prepared her for. She carried that experience through adolescence, learning traditional methods largely through trial and error.

As a young adult, she was introduced to natural horsemanship and began training with a master trainer in an NH program, filling gaps left by years of earlier, difficult lessons. She began helping rehab retired racehorses for rehoming through a nonprofit, which led to founding and facilitating a large trail riding and event group as a trail coach. From there she became a certified riding instructor with CHA, giving individual arena and trail lessons, running trail rides, and facilitating youth horse camps.

Her methods have evolved into gentler techniques that consider the whole horse. Alongside teaching Connected Horsemanship, she stays active in patterned speed events, competitive cow work and a little jumping, and continues studying LIMA, attunement, biomechanics, liberty work, obstacle courses, ranch work, and stock horse and ranch riding. No matter the discipline, there's always room for Connected Horsemanship.
